# Account and Billing

The desktop app's local features—terminals, workspaces, agents, LAN access, and Cloudflare Quick Tunnel—work without an account. Hosted Mobile companion services require an account and an active plan.

## Sign in and connect a desktop

1. In the desktop app, open **Settings → Mobile → Account**.
2. Choose whether to sign in to an existing account or follow the available option to start a trial, then enter your email address.
3. Open the one-time sign-in link sent to that address. You can open it on this computer or on your phone.
4. Return to the desktop app and select **Sign in on this computer** to finish. Opening the email link confirms the address; you still need to complete the sign-in in the app.

There is no account password. If you open the link on another device, keep the desktop app open and return to the Account page to finish signing in there. Follow the current options shown in the app for plan and trial availability.

## Review your plan and devices

In **Settings → Mobile → Account**, you can see the current plan and its renewal, trial-end, or cancellation date, along with account-wide desktop and phone usage. You can remove a desktop or phone from the account there. Removing a desktop ends its hosted access but leaves its local terminals and workspaces intact.

To connect a phone, use **Settings → Mobile → Phones** after signing in. The Account page's **Connect phone** button also takes you to the pairing controls.

## Manage billing

For an active subscription, choose **Manage billing** on the Account page. It opens the billing portal in your browser, where you can update payment details, view invoices, or cancel. The Account page shows the next renewal or cancellation date. After cancellation, hosted Mobile access remains available until the displayed cancellation date and the subscription will not renew.
